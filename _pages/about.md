About Me
======

I am a Ph.D. candidate majoring in [Social & Engineering Systems and Statistics](https://idss.mit.edu/academics/ses_doc/) at MIT IDSS and LIDS. I have been fortunately working with [Prof. Marzyeh Ghassemi](https://healthyml.org/marzyeh/) on machine learning for healthcare.

My current research centers around two primary topics:
* **LLM Safety and Alignment**: Designing algorithms and investigating underlying mechanisms to ensure safe, robust, and interpretable LLM behaviors and trustworthy deployment in high-stakes domains.
* **LLM Reasoning and Decision-Making**: Advancing LLM reasoning, agentic planning, and deep research capabilities through reinforcement learning to address complex decision-making challenges in healthcare and beyond.

For more information about my background and qualifications, please refer to [my CV](https://xiaoyuxin1002.github.io/files/CV.pdf).
